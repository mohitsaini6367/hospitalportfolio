While you reach us: first aid

Basic steps for accidents. They never replace a doctor, so move towards care at the same time.

Head or neck injury
  • Keep the person still. Do not move the neck or head.
  • Press a clean cloth on any bleeding.
  • Give nothing to eat or drink.
  • Get to the emergency room straight away if they were unconscious, vomit, or are confused.
Heavy bleeding
  • Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and keep pressing.
  • Do not remove soaked cloth; add more on top.
  • Raise the injured limb if it is not broken.
Suspected broken bone
  • Do not try to straighten it.
  • Keep the limb steady with a rolled cloth or splint.
  • Avoid food and drink in case surgery is needed.
